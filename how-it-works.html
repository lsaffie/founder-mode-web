How it works

A custom AI agent for your inbox and calendar.

Every morning it reads your email and calendar, does the work it can, and gives you a short briefing with the rest. We build it by hand around your week, and review it with you every month. We take on 10 founders a quarter, because each agent is built this way.

01  The service

A custom service, not software

You do not set this up. We build your agent by hand around your week, check it before it reaches you, and keep it accurate as your work changes. We take on 10 founders a quarter, because each agent is built this way.

  1. Discovery

    A conversation about your week

    How your days run, who matters, what you want off your plate, and what the briefing should leave out.

  2. Connection

    Fifteen minutes, together

    You connect your accounts on a call. You approve every permission yourself.

  3. Private test runs

    Checked before it reaches you

    We run the agent on your real data with nothing sent, drafted or changed, and review the output before your first briefing.

  4. First weeks

    Tuned to your standard

    We adjust it from your replies and our own review until it reads the way you would want a chief of staff to write.

  5. Every month

    A review call

    What is working, what to change, and whether the agent is still doing what you need.

  6. As you grow

    New connections and new agents

    When you want it to reach another system or take on new work, we scope it with you as a custom project.

No self-serve version, no free trial, by application only. The person who builds your agent is the person you meet on the monthly call.

06  Built for you

No two agents are alike

Every agent starts with a conversation about your week, and is built for you alone. These are the settings we tune together. Any of them can change when your work does.

Which days

Weekdays only, every day, or just the days you choose. The first briefing after a break covers everything you missed.

What it covers

Your whole day, or work only. Work-only leaves out family, health and household, but still flags a legal or financial deadline.

Who you are

Your role, your companies and your key relationships, so it describes every person and company correctly.

For example: which companies are yours, which are investments, which are clients.

What it replies to

Whether it drafts replies, and for whom. Up to five a day, in your voice, never sent.

For example: never draft for recruiters or vendors.

Your calendar

Read-only, or allowed to add meetings you have already agreed to. It adds an event only when the time is explicit.

Weekly routines

Recurring reminders on the days you pick, placed at the end of that day’s actions.

For example: Monday, review the pipeline with Sales.

Your task list

In Notion, using your own statuses, priorities and categories. It explains every change it makes.

Engineering activity

The GitHub repositories to watch. It reports shipped work only when it changes something for you.

Talking back

Whether you can reply to the briefing and have the agent act on what you write.

What to leave out

Topics, senders and threads you never want to see again. Stated once, applied every day after.

A founder with a co-founder

His briefing leaves out every thread his COO already owns, so it only shows what reaches him.

A deal-driven CEO

Hers opens with the chasers written overnight, because her day turns on who has gone quiet.

A technical founder

His watches five repositories and reports shipped work only when it changes a customer answer.

07  Control

It earns access one step at a time

The agent only has the abilities you have switched on. Anything you have not switched on, it cannot do.

1

Reads

It reads your email and calendar and writes your briefing. It changes nothing.

2

Drafts

It writes replies and follow-ups into your Drafts folder. You decide what gets sent.

3

Acts

It adds calendar events and updates your task list, with a written reason for every change.

Every client starts at step one. Each step up is your decision, and you can step back at any time.

08  Privacy

Your inbox stays yours

You are giving an AI agent access to your email. Here is what that means, in plain terms.

We never see your password

You approve access yourself, on your own screen, on a short call.

It never sends email as you

Replies wait in your Drafts folder. The only email it sends is your briefing, to you.

Your email never trains AI

Anthropic, which runs the AI, does not train on it and deletes it within 30 days.

You can cut off access in one click

From your Google or Microsoft account settings, at any time.

Leave whenever you like, and we delete everything we hold. Your mailbox, calendar and tasks stay exactly as they are. They were always yours.

How much access does it have?

To draft replies and deliver your briefing, the agent needs full access to your mailbox. We say so plainly.

Who reads my briefings?

You, and the person who builds your agent, who reads them to tune it. No one else.

What does it keep while you are a client?

A short working memory: your recent briefings, what you have told it, and the follow-ups it is tracking. It is stored encrypted and never shared.

What leaves my account?

Email and calendar content goes to the AI model to write your briefing. For meeting research, the names of outside people and companies are searched on the public web.

One screen to expect when you connect a Google account

Google will show a notice that it has not verified the app. Verification is a process built for mass-market apps; Founder Mode is a private service for a small number of clients.

We walk you through that screen on the call.

09  Fit

Who this is for

We take a few founders at a time, so we are careful about who we take.

A good fit

  • Your day runs on email and calendar

    What lands in your inbox and what sits on your calendar decides how your day goes.

  • You will hand over real access

    The agent earns its keep by reading your actual mail, not a summary of it.

  • You will say when it is wrong

    The first weeks are tuning. Your corrections are what make month six better than month one.

Not a fit

  • Software you set up yourself

    There is no self-serve version, no free trial and no app to install.

  • A tool for the whole team

    One agent serves one person. It is your chief of staff, not a shared inbox.

  • Everyone who applies

    We take on 10 founders a quarter and say no to the rest, because each agent is built by hand.
